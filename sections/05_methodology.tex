\subsection{Hardware Platforms}

% P:meth-platforms
Table~\ref{tab:params} lists both platforms. Each platform follows the published setup of the rival that targets it: the mobile platform takes its x64
LPDDR5-6400 and INT8 precision from LP-Spec~\cite{lpspec}, and the edge server takes its NPU, HBM2 and W4A8 precision
from P3-LLM~\cite{p3llm}. The mobile platform pairs the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3~\cite{sd8g3} host and NPU, in place of
LP-Spec's modelled NPU, with PIM on the LPDDR5-6400. The NPU computes at its measured FP16 GEMM rate~\cite{hao2025},
used for INT8\ASSUMPTION, and reads the memory at the bandwidth of Section~\ref{sec:bg:roofline}. Each host visit costs
30\,\textmu s of NPU dispatch~\cite{npufaster}\ASSUMPTION; the host draws 2.285\,W while busy,
the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3's~\cite{sd8g3} decode power net of its DRAM~\cite{hao2025,heterollm,tman}\ASSUMPTION. The edge server pairs
P3-LLM's NPU, priced at its peak rate\ASSUMPTION, with one HBM2 stack; the NPU idles at 55\,W, the chip idle power of TPUv4i~\cite{tpuv4i},
while the PIM works\ASSUMPTION.

\subsection{PIM Designs}
\label{sec:method:designs}
% P:meth-designs
Each substrate carries four designs, stock AiM~\cite{aimjssc}, P3-LLM-style, LP-Spec-style and CoDe, with one
controller and the platform's PU placement. Stock AiM also stands for Samsung's per-bank
LPDDR5X-PIM~\cite{lp5xpim}. Table~\ref{tab:designs} lists each design's lanes, PU clock, beats per
column period $D$, tile size $V$, accumulator slots and GB size. Every design computes at its
platform's precision (Table~\ref{tab:params}), with a 4-bit KV cache on HBM2, and every design's arithmetic is
priced at that precision, INT8 on the mobile platform and INT4\,$\times$\,INT8 on HBM2\ASSUMPTION. Sections~\ref{sec:design:pu} and~\ref{sec:eval:ablation}
size $V$.

\subsection{Simulator and Models}
\label{sec:method:models}
% P:meth-simulator
We extend the open-source trace-driven AiM simulator, built on Ramulator~2.0~\cite{ramulator2} and used by
CENT~\cite{cent}, with each design's commands and a PU model that holds a MAC command until the PU has finished the
beats of the previous command; every PIM kernel runs at cycle level. Our P3-LLM-style model reproduces, and slightly
exceeds, P3-LLM's~\cite{p3llm} own step gain of depth 2 over a depth-1 PIM. On the mobile platform the simulator issues an all-bank refresh every $t_\mathrm{REFI}$ and blocks every command to
the rank for $t_\mathrm{RFCab}$, the JEDEC values of a 16\,Gb die (Table~\ref{tab:params}), which we assume for our
dies\ASSUMPTION.

% P:meth-area-model
Logic area comes from synthesis of each PU's arithmetic and registers with Synopsys Design Compiler~\cite{synopsysdc} (compile\_ultra
with DesignWare~\cite{synopsysdw}) on Synopsys' SAED14 educational 14\,nm library~\cite{saed14} (RVT cells, typical corner at 0.8\,V and 25\,$^\circ$C),
multiplied by ten for the tenfold density penalty UPMEM~\cite{devaux} reports for logic in a DRAM
process\ASSUMPTION. Registers are priced per bit from synthesized register blocks, each PU adds a fixed control area,
and the GB is priced as DRAM-process SRAM\ASSUMPTION. PrimeTime~\cite{synopsyspt} confirms that each synthesized PU meets timing at its
clock of Table~\ref{tab:designs}. Die shares divide the logic area by a 60.92\,mm$^2$ 16\,Gb LPDDR5 die, as
LP-Spec~\cite{lpspec} reports, and on HBM2 by a stack of eight 84.4\,mm$^2$ dies, the die area of
HBM-PIM~\cite{hbmpim}, which stands in for a plain HBM2 die\ASSUMPTION.

% P:meth-power-model
PrimePower~\cite{synopsyspp} computes PU power and energy from the Design Compiler netlist of each whole PU, placed and
routed in IC Compiler II~\cite{synopsysicc2} and simulated in VCS~\cite{synopsysvcs} with back-annotated timing on random operands. Energy and leakage are scaled to the DRAM process so that an AiM-style bf16 PU from the same flow spends
the compute share of CENT's~\cite{cent} AiM MAC-command energy, and to the 1.05\,V VDD2H supply with the
square of the voltage\ASSUMPTION. Energy per token charges each MAC its PU's energy, leakage over
the step time, GB reads priced by SRAM size~\cite{horowitz}, and CENT's per-command DRAM energies; in
every design a PU's clock runs only on its MAC beats, so an idle PU cycle costs leakage only\ASSUMPTION. Peak power
runs every PU at its full MAC rate and includes leakage, GB reads and the column fetch, which adds 87.6\,mW per die on
the mobile platform and 54.8\,mW per pseudo-channel on HBM2 to every design. P3-LLM-style, LP-Spec-style and CoDe
clock-gate their accumulator slots and staging words, and CoDe its weight latch. As yardsticks, one x16 LPDDR5X channel reading at its full rate draws 447\,mW by its
datasheet currents~\cite{micronlp5x}, and an HBM2 stack reading at its full rate draws 8.0\,W at
3.92\,pJ/bit~\cite{oconnor}.

\subsection{Algorithms and Workloads}
\label{sec:method:work}
% P:meth-workloads
The mobile platform runs speculative decoding of Llama-2-7B in INT8 with a 1.1B-parameter draft model: $M - 1$
drafted tokens and one verify pass of $M$ rows, acceptance rate 0.7 per token and context $L = 1024$. The edge server runs batched decoding of
Llama-2-7B and 13B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B and Mistral-7B at a 4K context, one token per request per step. GEMMs
use six FC shapes of Llama-2-7B and Llama-3.2-1B on the mobile platform and the 25 FC shapes of the five models on the
edge server. End to end, every system runs the edge server's batch at $M = 8$ and the mobile loop at $M = 8$: seven drafted
tokens within the 2--8 that published mobile decoders verify~\cite{sdnpu}, so that the eight verify rows fill one
eight-token tile of CoDe.

% P:meth-systems
NPU only runs the whole model on the NPU; in NPU + $X$, PIM design $X$ runs every weight GEMM and attention layer and
the NPU runs the element-wise work (softmax, RoPE and normalisation). Every PIM design maps a $K \times N$ weight
matrix as stock AiM does: the matrix's $N$ output rows spread over the banks, and each row's $K$ weights run along DRAM
columns; attention maps the same way, with the KV cache as the weight matrix. The one exception is NPU + P3-LLM-style on the edge server, which follows the
placement of P3-LLM~\cite{p3llm} at batch 8: the NPU runs the linear layers and P3-LLM-style the attention layers.

\subsection{Metrics}
% P:meth-metrics
GEMM speed counts a GEMM's operations over its time including the drain of its output sums, relative to stock AiM;
end-to-end speed is tokens per second, relative to NPU only. Area efficiency divides
either throughput by the PIM logic area over all dies or pseudo-channels of Table~\ref{tab:designs}; it is a primary
metric beside speed, because a DRAM die pays for logic in memory capacity (Section~\ref{sec:bg:walls}). Energy is system
energy per token. Edge-server values are geometric means over the five models (Llama-2-7B and 13B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B and
Mistral-7B).
